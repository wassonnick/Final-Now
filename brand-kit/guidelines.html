Colour

Near-white canvases and Charcoal Ink do the work; a single Verified Green is the only chromatic voice — it marks CTAs, links and anything reviewed. Amber appears in-product purely as a caution / “estimated” signal and is never a brand colour.

Typography

One family across the identity: Hanken Grotesk, a warm geometric sans. Headlines run 600 weight with tight tracking (−0.02em); body sits at 400 with generous line-height; uppercase labels take +5% letter-spacing. No serif anywhere. Free Google Font (OFL) — install it before producing print files.

The same family carries the interface: labels, buttons, numbers, captions — 400/500/600/700/800.

The mark

A society grid — nine homes on a Charcoal Ink tile, one cell in Verified Green (the home you find) and one taller cell (the doorway). Clear space: keep one-third of the tile width around it. Never recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.

Lockups

Horizontal is the default. The standalone wordmark is reserved for layouts where the symbol already appears nearby. Stacked lockups are for square placements and stamps. Reversed variants belong on Charcoal Ink or sufficiently dark photography.

Social

Sized to each platform's current spec with safe areas respected. Photo/video zones are placeholders — drop real society photography (verified societies only) and keep one clay CTA per layout.

Print

Built at 300dpi equivalents. Business card includes 0.125in bleed on every edge — give printers the SVG/PDF export and this page. Print in CMYK: Estate Green ≈ C85 M45 Y70 K45, Clay ≈ C15 M55 Y80 K5.

Rules

  • Voice: a knowledgeable local friend — warm, specific, honest. No hype, no exclamation marks, no "luxury living at its finest".
  • Claims: only verified data. "Admin-verified" and "no fake inventory" are the brand promise — never dilute them with unverifiable claims.
  • Jade means verified or connected — do not use it for warnings, pricing or decoration without meaning.
  • One gold accent per layout — the verified signal or a single editorial emphasis. If everything is gold, nothing is.
  • Photography: real societies, daylight, uncluttered. Never stock towers that aren't ours to show.
  • Minimum mark size: 24px digital / 8mm print. Below that, use the tile mark alone, never the lockups.
  • Backgrounds: white or Surface for light layouts; Charcoal Ink for dark identity layouts. Never place the mark on busy photography without its tile.
  • Don't: stretch, recolour, outline, shadow, rotate, or pair with other marks without clear space.
